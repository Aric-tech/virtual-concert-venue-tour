# Image credits

The exterior panorama is a blended 360-degree background assembled from two photographs. They were taken at different locations around Marina Bay; the panorama gives continuous visual coverage but is not a single surveyed viewpoint.

- `marina-bay-night-panorama.jpg` — Rocks n' Scapes, “Panorama of Singapore's Marina Bay”, [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Panorama_of_Singapore%27s_Marina_Bay.jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Cropped, rescaled and blended into the exterior panorama.
- `marina-bay-city-panorama.jpg` — Romain Pontida, “Singapore Marina Bay at night (20343670063)”, [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Singapore_Marina_Bay_at_night_(20343670063).jpg), [CC BY-SA 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.0/). Cropped, rescaled and blended into the exterior panorama.
- `marina-bay-sands-frontal-night.jpg` — DvTor8303, “Marina Bay Sands at night 20250904 (2)”, [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Marina_Bay_Sands_at_night_20250904_(2).jpg), [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). A frontal hotel view blended over the oblique hotel portion of the exterior panorama.
